Request data deletion

Forms App by Firebirdapps · Last updated: 3 November 2026

You can ask us to delete identifiable feedback and support information that we hold for Forms App (com.firebirdapps.forms_app). You do not need to sign in to the app to send a request.

Send your deletion request to firebirdapps.dev@gmail.com with the subject Forms App data deletion request.

How to request deletion

  1. Open your email app and send a message to the address above.
  2. Tell us whether you want to delete feedback submitted in Forms App, previous support messages, or both.
  3. For in-app feedback, include the approximate submission date, star rating, and a short excerpt of your message so we can locate it. Feedback submissions do not include your Google email or account identifier. For support messages, use the email address you originally contacted us from and identify the relevant conversation.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, OAuth tokens, or private form answers. We may ask for limited additional information to locate a record or verify a request. If we cannot identify the requested data, we will explain this rather than claim it has been deleted.

Data covered by your request

  • Identifiable in-app feedback: the matching Firebase Realtime Database submission, including the feedback text, star rating, submission identifier, timestamp, app version/build, platform, and other metadata stored with that record.
  • Identifiable support communications: the messages and attachments we hold in the support conversations covered by your request, subject to any necessary legal retention described below.

Deletion requests are handled manually. After locating the records and completing any necessary verification, we will confirm the outcome by email. Sending a request does not itself immediately delete database records.

Retention and information that may remain

Feedback does not have an automatic expiry, and the app does not automatically delete user data within 90 days. Feedback and support information may otherwise remain while needed to investigate requests, improve the app, or meet legal requirements. There is no routine additional retention period for the records we delete after completing a verified request.

If a legal obligation requires us to retain particular information, we retain only what is necessary for that obligation and only for the required period. We will explain any applicable exception and retention period when responding to your request, unless the law prevents us from doing so.

Firebase Analytics: the app records usage events and an app-instance identifier. It does not attach your Google account email or identifier to Analytics events, so we cannot locate those records using your Google email alone. Deleting feedback or a support conversation does not delete Analytics records. Identifiable Analytics records are subject to the project's Google Analytics retention settings and Google's service policies; a fixed expiry is not implemented in the app. Aggregate usage reports that cannot be associated with an identified user may remain without a fixed retention limit. If your request concerns Analytics, mention this in your email so we can explain the available options.

Google Forms, device data, and shared copies

  • Your Google account, forms, and responses: these stay in Google's services. We do not create a separate Forms App account or keep a developer-hosted archive of your live forms and answers. Manage or delete forms through Google Forms or Google Drive; moving a form to trash is different from permanently deleting it. An email request to us does not delete your Google account or its files.
  • Google authorization: use Disconnect Google account in Forms App or remove the connection in Google Account settings. This revokes access; it does not delete your forms, feedback, or Analytics records.
  • Local preferences, demo forms, and app caches: use Android Settings → Apps → Forms App → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device). This removes local app data and unsaved work. Android backups may restore some preferences later. Signing out clears the active notification account and response checkpoints, but does not clear every preference or cached export.
  • Exported, printed, or shared copies: remove them from the storage location, recipient app, print service, or other destination you chose. We cannot remove copies held by those destinations or by recipients. Google Play reviews must be managed through Google Play.

For more information, read the Forms App Privacy policy. Privacy and deletion contact: Firebirdapps, firebirdapps.dev@gmail.com.
